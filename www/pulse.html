🐋 Whale TransfersLarge exchange inflow / outflow · Multi-chain
How this works
Tracks large native / stablecoin moves in or out of a curated set of known exchange wallets per chain (threshold ≈ $250K). Inflow to an exchange is often a sell signal; outflow to cold storage is often accumulation. Wallet lists are a small, best-effort sample — not exhaustive, and easy to get subtly wrong, especially for Bitcoin & Solana. Verify any address you rely on and add your own via the panel above.
🐦 Crypto X PulseWhales, exchange founders & VCs to watch
Public feeds, best-effort reliability — not a trading venue.
Whale transfers come from three places depending on the chain: Etherscan V2 for Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Arbitrum & Base (requires your own free Etherscan API key — one key covers all of these); mempool.space for Bitcoin (no key); and Solana's public RPC for Solana (no key). The EOA exchange wallet list is reused across all EVM chains since those addresses aren't chain-specific; Bitcoin and Solana each have their own small, editable, best-effort address list — not comprehensive, and worth double-checking yourself before relying on it. The X/Twitter feed no longer uses Nitter-style mirrors — those were shut down for good after a cease-and-desist from X Corp in August 2026 (the project's own repository was archived days later). It now pulls from X's own public syndication/embed feed instead, the free JSON endpoint that normally powers "Embedded Timelines" on other websites. That feed isn't officially documented for this kind of use and X throttles or empties it for unauthenticated requests on and off, so treat it as best-effort — when it comes back empty, this section falls back to direct profile links instead of failing silently. This page doesn't place trades or hold positions. Nothing here is financial advice.
