TVL figures follow DefiLlama's methodology (assets deposited into a protocol's smart contracts, whether they be lending markets, DEX pools, staking contracts, etc.). "TVL" is not the same as a protocol's market cap or revenue, and double-counting can occur when protocols compose on top of each other. DEX volume reflects on-chain trade volume reported by each DEX's own adapter, not order-book/CEX activity. Unlock and emission-schedule data is thinner than TVL data industry-wide — DefiLlama moved its full unlock calendar behind a paid Pro plan, so this page shows whatever the free tier still exposes and links out to dedicated unlock trackers as a fallback. Figures refresh roughly every 10 minutes; DefiLlama itself typically updates TVL every few hours.
