Sources checked & methodology
    Dates come from primary sources (Federal Reserve, BLS, Census, BEA, ISM, Treasury, OPEC, official event sites, Congress.gov trackers) as of the "last verified" date shown in the banner at the top. Legislative, upgrade, and estimated-cadence dates in particular can shift — items marked Tentative have no locked date yet, and items with a ✓ have been directly confirmed against a primary source. This page has no backend, so it can't check the web itself on a schedule — it re-verifies only when someone edits this file (you, or Claude on request) after checking the live sources above. The banner turns amber after 7 days without a check and red after 14, as a nudge to ask for a re-verify rather than a guarantee anything changed. Countdowns and Live/Upcoming/Past sorting auto-refresh every 30 seconds in the background (or on tap of Refresh) for as long as the page stays open, but that only re-sorts today's data — it doesn't fetch anything new. Weekly initial jobless claims (every Thursday, 8:30am ET, from the Department of Labor) aren't listed as individual entries since they recur weekly — check dol.gov for the latest. DOGE and XRP already trade via several US spot ETFs launched in late 2025/early 2026, so this calendar tracks upcoming catalysts (conferences, network upgrades, macro prints) rather than day-to-day ETF flows for those two. Re-check primary sources before trading around any single date.
