How to read this
  • X-axis, days since peak. Day 0 is the cycle's peak, so every cycle starts at the same point and the paths can be compared directly.
  • Y-axis, ROI. The close as a multiple of the peak close: 1 is the peak, 0.6 is 40% below it, 2 is double. The axis is logarithmic, so equal vertical distances are equal percentage moves.
  • The idea. The 2019 and 2025 peaks both came as quantitative tightening (QT, the central bank shrinking its balance sheet) was running down. Some traders therefore treat the 2019 path as a template for 2025. The overlay lets you check that for yourself, and against other cycles too.
  • Same-day ROI and what happened next. The table shows each past cycle on the same day count as today, and how it moved over the following 90 and 180 days. That is history, not a forecast.
  • Similarity. Correlation compares the shape of the paths (1 means they move together), and average gap is how far apart the two lines sat on average. A high correlation on trending data is easy to get, so lean on the gap too.
  • Limits. There are only a few cycles. They had different causes (the 2020 rally came after a crash and heavy stimulus). A matching path so far does not mean it continues to match.
