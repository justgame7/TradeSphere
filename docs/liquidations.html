Reference data only, not financial advice.
Ratio/open interest/funding figures come from Binance Futures public endpoints (USDⓈ-M perpetuals) and reflect Binance's own order book and traders only — not the whole market. "Top trader" ratios cover Binance's top 20% of accounts/positions by margin balance, which behaves differently from the retail-heavy "global accounts" ratio, so the two can diverge or even point opposite directions. The Liquidation Totals section (1h/8h/24h per coin) comes from the free Coinalyze API, aggregated across the exchanges Coinalyze tracks — it needs a free API key (see the section itself). Ratio/open interest/funding refresh every 10 minutes; Liquidation Totals every 5 minutes.
