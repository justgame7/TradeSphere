ETF Flows

Daily net creations and redemptions across every approved US spot crypto ETF — Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana and Hyperliquid — sourced from public fund-issuer flow disclosures.

Reference data only, not investment advice.
Flow figures are in US$ millions and are aggregated from public fund-issuer disclosures. They typically post once daily in the evening/night (US time) and are not real-time — today's column can read "—" until the day's flows are reported. A fund not yet listed by an issuer, or with no ETF approved for that asset, simply won't appear. "Total" is the cumulative net flow since each ETF's launch date, not a market-cap figure. Not financial advice.
